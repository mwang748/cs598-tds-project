# Rental-car selection: four draft scenarios

All company names, prices, availability and transfer arrangements below are fictional study details, not actual Boston rental offers.

## Question order

1. All four flight-selection questions.
2. All four combined hotel/room/rate questions.
3. All four rental-car-selection questions.
4. The existing four flight-reservation preparation questions.

The current draft has 16 tasks. Within each group, conditions run tight budget/fixed schedule, tight budget/flexible schedule, higher budget/fixed schedule, higher budget/flexible schedule. Assumptions belong at the start of each task group, not on the trip overview.

## Shared inventory

All rentals last 72 hours, with pickup and return at 10 a.m. ET at the same location. Each price includes taxes and mandatory fees. Shuttles are free; times include waiting, each way. All cars meet the traveler's space requirements and are automatic. Companies are equally acceptable; coverage, cancellation, mileage and fuel policies are identical. The preference is shorter pickup/return transfers, balanced against savings.

Fixed dates mean Friday–Monday. Flexible dates allow any listed rental period with no date preference. Whole-trip shifts cause no additional flight or hotel costs, and tasks are independent of earlier answers. Every rental still lasts 72 hours.

A is manual search and choice. B lists every eligible rental, alphabetically grouped by company, without a recommendation. C recommends a shortlist and leaves the final decision to the participant. D selects without approval and displays both the selected package and any alternatives named in its comparison. Excluded companies are not introduced in surrounding text.

The fixed-schedule budget contrast is now 3 versus 8 eligible rentals. Under $240 only downtown options qualify; under $450, airport and near-airport choices become available. No condition guarantees a different participant answer.

## Full participant drafts

### 1. Tight budget, tight schedule

#### Task instructions

Rent a car in Boston, arriving and leaving through Logan Airport. Prefer convenient airport pickup/return, but consider a longer transfer to save money.

- Budget: $240 total, including taxes and mandatory fees.
- Dates: Friday–Monday.
- Pickup/return: 10 a.m. ET, same location; 72 hours.

Company names are fictional. You qualify to rent; all cars are automatic and fit you and your luggage. Companies are equally acceptable; coverage and rental terms are identical. Shuttles are free; times include waiting.

Independent of earlier flight/hotel answers; either location is reachable by pickup. Nothing is booked or paid.

#### How would you prefer to find and choose your rental car?

##### A. Search and choose myself

I search rental websites, compare the cars, locations, and dates, and choose without the chatbot.

##### B. Chatbot searches; I choose

Here are 3 matching rentals across 3 companies, listed by company. Prices cover all 3 days, including taxes and mandatory fees.

- City Rentals · Compact — Friday–Monday: $220 | Downtown Boston; 25-minute shuttle each way.
- Harbor Rentals · Compact — Friday–Monday: $210 | Downtown Boston; 35-minute shuttle each way.
- West Rentals · Midsize — Friday–Monday: $235 | Downtown Boston; 35-minute shuttle each way.

Compare and choose. I have not ranked or recommended them.

##### C. Chatbot recommends a shortlist; I choose

I found 3 matching rentals. Here are 2 I recommend comparing:

- Harbor Rentals · Compact — Friday–Monday: $210 | Downtown Boston; 35-minute shuttle each way.
- City Rentals · Compact — Friday–Monday: $220 | Downtown Boston; 25-minute shuttle each way.

City Rentals costs $10 more than Harbor Rentals but saves 10 minutes of shuttle time each way. You make the final choice.

##### D. Chatbot chooses without asking me

I selected City Rentals · Compact, Friday–Monday, for $220. Here are the compared packages:

- City Rentals · Compact — Friday–Monday: $220 | Downtown Boston; 25-minute shuttle each way.

It costs $10 more than Harbor Rentals but saves 10 minutes of shuttle time each way. I make the selection without asking for approval. No reservation or payment is made.
